    History rules review: core rules¶

    Scope: the events the Draw Steel core rules create (Heroes book Ch. 1 The Basics, Ch. 5 general ability rules and conditions, Ch. 9 Tests, Ch. 10 Combat, Ch. 11 Negotiation, Ch. 12 Downtime Projects, the glossary, and the general Heroic Resource pattern). Class features and monster/Director rules are covered by the other two reviews. Written 2026-11-04 against branch feature/hero-sheet-party-picker.

    How to read this¶

    • Rule cites H:<line> = ClaudeBridge/ClaudeRefHeroes.lua.
    • Codex today cites code with folder shorthand: DGR/ = DMHub Game Rules/, DSCR/ = Draw Steel Core Rules/, DSUI/ = Draw Steel UI/, DSV/ = Draw Steel V/, DT/ = Downtime Projects/, data/ = data/objectTables/. Most rules are global rule mods (data/globalrulemods/*.yaml) or condition rows (data/charconditions/*.yaml) implemented as triggered abilities.
    • Logged today? is the Action Log (ChatPanel/ActionLogPanel.lua, which shows every non-chat message: rolls and custom messages). Three things decide whether an automatic rule shows up:
    • a triggered ability posts a cast card only when mandatory is false (prompted) (DGR/TriggeredAbility.lua:2644); automatic rule triggers are silent;
    • a damage/replenish/purge/heal behavior posts its own card only when its chatMessage field is set (Draw Steel Ability Behaviors/AbilityDamage.lua:56, DGR/AbilityReplenish.lua:718, DGR/AbilityPurgeEffects.lua:286, DGR/ActivatedAbility.lua:5136);
    • per-target notes recorded on a cast card (RecordTokenMessage) render only as hover tooltips, and not at all for the caster (comment at DGR/ActivatedAbility.lua:5138).
    • Several silent changes are still recorded somewhere, just not in the log: per-stat histories (creature:GetStatHistory, DGR/Creature.lua:1899; stamina, temporary stamina, max stamina, every resource) shown as tooltips on the sheet, the global resource history for Hero Tokens and Malice (DGR/Resource.lua:40), the negotiation's own story log (DSV/NegotiationRules.lua:692), the montage tally (DocumentSystem/MontageDocument.lua:77) and the downtime project roll list (DT/DTProject.lua). History can read these instead of starting from nothing.
    • History should show uses the brief's row types: Banner (combat start/end), Round heading, Turn heading, Action row, Nested row (a triggered or automatic effect under the action that set it off), Turn line (compact line with no action: start/end of turn, saves, conditions ending; COPY T16 format), What happened sentence and Why line on the detail card (COPY N2/N3, R1 wording). Play outside combat (tests, montage, negotiation, respite) has no rounds, so it is shown as Scene rows (an action row grouped under the scene instead of a turn).
    • How often: every combat / often / rare.

    Count¶

    130 events across 15 topics (counted from the tables above):

    • 23 have no implementation found in the codex (20 not found, 3 not verified).
    • 46 are automated, or done by hand in the app, but leave no Action Log entry. Some of these are kept in a stat history, the Hero Token history or the negotiation's own log.
    • 34 have a log entry for the event itself. Several are bare, for example "Collision 2" with no push, or a temporary Stamina card that can be wrong.
    • 27 leave only a partial trace: a roll with no outcome, an edge/bane count, a tooltip, or a card for a different part of the event.

    Gaps that matter most¶

    1. Forced movement and what it caused. Push/pull/slide has no record, and the parts that change it (stability, Big Versus Little, a full negation) live only in the move prompt or a speech bubble. Collision and fall damage appear as bare cards with no link to the push. Every combat.
    2. Winded, dying, death and kills. Nothing is logged, and dying silently adds bleeding that will hurt the Hero on every main action. Every combat.
    3. End of turn. EoT effects end silently; a saving throw shows only its d10, not what it ended. Every combat.
    4. Damage with no ability. Aura, damaging terrain and start-of-turn damage reach only the stamina history; hand edits to Stamina are invisible. Often.
    5. Conditions ending on their own. A grab ended by a teleport, hidden ended by attacking, surprise ending in round 2, condition immunity (speech bubble only): no record. Often.
    6. End-of-combat cleanup. Heroic Resource reset, surges and temporary Stamina lost, EoE effects ended, Victories awarded: all silent. Every combat.
    7. Hero Token spends. The surge and Stamina spends sit only in the hidden token history; a token-saved saving throw looks like a failed roll. Often.
    8. Test consequences and rewards. The default "+2 Malice next combat" and "Hero Token" outcomes aren't automated or recorded, so the Malice at the next combat has no visible cause. Often outside combat.

    Surprises¶

    • Hurled through a wall deals no damage. Breakable walls break, but nothing deals the rules' 3/5/8/11 damage (AbilityRelocateCreature.lua:669).
    • Save-ends effects outlive combat. creature:EndCombat clears only EoE durations, but the rules say save ends lasts until a save or the end of combat (H:937-939).
    • The temporary Stamina card can be wrong. It reports the granted amount even when the creature keeps a larger existing pool.
    • Potency results are already recorded per target, but only as cast-card tooltips.
    • A flying creature reduced to speed 0 (grabbed, restrained) does not fall; only prone triggers a fall.
    • Some rule-driven records already exist outside the log. These are the stat histories, the Hero Token and Malice history, the negotiation's toned story log, the montage tally and the project roll list. History can read them instead of rebuilding.
    • Frightened and taunted end when their source dies. This is a codex behaviour, and it is logged; the Heroes condition text I read does not include it.
    • The Elementalist example (class review's scope): H:11159-11160. The rule reads "If you take damage equal to or greater than 5 times your Reason score in one turn, you stop maintaining any persistent abilities."
