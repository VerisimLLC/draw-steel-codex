TEMPLE OF SAINT LLEWELLYN -- ALL PLAYER-FACING TEXT (placeholder drafts, 2026-11-12)

HOW TO EDIT
  * Each entry is two lines: the id in [square brackets], then the words.
  * Change only the words. Leave every [id] exactly as it is.
  * {name}, {goal}, etc. are filled in by the game. Keep each one, spelled the
    same; you may move it anywhere in the sentence.
  * Plain keyboard characters only: straight quotes ' and ", three dots ...,
    a hyphen or colon for a dash. No curly quotes or em dashes.
  * A line starting with # is a note for you; it is not shown in the game.
  * To leave a roll line unwritten, leave its words empty.
When you are done, send this file back and the changes go into the game's
text files (EotwTextTemple.lua and EotwTextTown.lua) by id.
