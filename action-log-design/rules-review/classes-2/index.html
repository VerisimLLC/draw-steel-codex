    History rules review: Shadow, Tactician, Talent, Troubadour, Beastheart, ancestries, kits¶

    Audit date 2026-11-04. Scope: class features, Heroic Resource rules, subclass features, ancestry traits and kit rules that fire on their own or change numbers without an action, plus abilities whose effect sets up a later automatic event. Ordinary abilities used as actions are skipped. Code and data were read, not run; nothing was checked in the app.

    Paths. R:n = ClaudeBridge/ClaudeRefHeroes.lua line n (the Heroes book as text). B:n = beastheart-reference.md line n (it sits at the repo root, not in Draw Steel Beastheart/). Data paths are under data/objectTables/: c/ classes, s/ subclasses, e/ characterongoingeffects, cd/ charconditions, anc/ races, k/ kits, a/ adventuretables, sa/ standardabilities, g/ globalrulemods; m/ is data/monsters/. The YAML line is the feature or trigger that does the work. Implementation tags quoted as Gold/Silver/Bronze/Unimpl are the data's own implementation field (4/3/2/1).

    Draft lines are proposals for sign-off, in COPY.md style (past tense, result first, names not pronouns, Hero and Stamina capitalised, conditions lower case inside sentences). Example names: Human Shadow, High Elf Tactician, Time Raider Talent, Polder Troubadour, Orc Beastheart and their companion Wolf.

    How the codex decides what reaches the log (same mechanism as classes-1.md)¶

    1. Automatic triggered abilities never post a cast card: TriggeredAbility.mandatory defaults to true (DMHub Game Rules/TriggeredAbility.lua:967) and ShowChatMessageOnCast() returns not self.mandatory (:2644). Heroic Resource triggers use the setting id game:heroicresourcetriggers (a truthy string), so they post no cast card either.
    2. An automatic event is visible only when one of its behaviors posts its own card, and nearly all of them do so only when the author typed a chatMessage (Replenish, Damage, Heal, Grant Temporary Stamina, Purge Effects). Heroic Resource gains linked to a class checklist item always post a resource line whose reason is the checklist name (DMHub Game Rules/AbilityReplenish.lua:661-728), which also gates them once per round/encounter (:348-366).
    3. Flat damage from an automatic trigger with no chatMessage is silent: the damage roll is deterministic, so the roll dialog resolves it with silent = true (Timeline/EmbeddedRollDialog.lua:6890-6936).
    4. Prompted triggers (mandatory: false) post a cast card when accepted; per-target results sit in its hover.
    5. Power-table triggered actions (behavior: powertabletrigger, e.g. Defensive Roll, Parry, Repel) are taken inside another creature's roll or damage dialog; they post nothing of their own (Draw Steel Core Rules/PowerTableTriggers.lua has no chat call).
    6. Passive edges, banes, damage bonuses and cost changes fold into the roll. The roll record keeps the kept modifier names (Draw Steel UI/DSRollDialog.lua:3963-3976) but no card shows them as a reason. Kit bonuses are folded into the ability text before the roll; the ability card says "Includes +2 damage from Shining Armor kit" (Draw Steel Core Rules/MCDMKit.lua:471-476), the log says nothing.
    7. Effects ending (by duration, on dying, at end of encounter, or by a Purge with no chatMessage) are never logged. Recoveries spent because a feature allowed it log as a bare "Use Recovery" resource line with no cause (g/triggers.yaml:14-51).

    Logged today codes. RES = resource line with the quoted reason. DMG = damage line with the quoted text. TMP = temporary Stamina line. HEAL = Stamina regained line. PURGE = effects removed line. CAST = cast card (ability used or prompted trigger accepted). ROLL = only inside a roll. CHIP = immunity/weakness chip (DamageModifierChatMessage). FLOAT = floating text over the token, not in the log. NO = nothing.

    Row types. Action = action row. Nested = triggered row under what set it off. Turn line / Resource line / Ended line = compact lines with no action. Why = detail-card Why line (lead / sentence, COPY.md R1).

    Gaps that matter most¶

    1. Effects ending are invisible. I'm No Threat ending when the Shadow harms someone, Careful Observation lost by striking another creature first, Fake Your Death ending on damage, Mind Projection snapping back, force orbs lost, Mark ending when the Tactician is dying, a performance ending because the Troubadour is dazed, Shadowmeld exits: all silent. This is the same class of gap as the Elementalist's persisting ability.
    2. Talent strain is half visible. End-of-turn negative-clarity damage is logged ("Strain damage"), but becoming and stopping being strained is not, and six strained riders that hurt the Talent (Mind Spike, Spirit Sword, Materialize, Flashback, Perfect Clarity, Gravitic Nova) have no damage text. Psion's opt-out is not implemented.
    3. Rampage thresholds are not announced. Reaching 8 (rampaging) or 12 (immunity) leaves no record; the 16, 20 and 24 effects are display-only and not automated; the mandatory end-of-turn Feral Strike is a prompt, so a declined prompt breaks the rule silently.
    4. Heroic Resource lines lack their cause. Every gain is logged, but only with a checklist name ("Damage the Marked", "A Creature is Force Moved"); the creature, roll or forced movement that caused it is not linked, and losses (end of encounter, Spotlight's unspent resource, Borrow From the Future's lockout) are not logged.
    5. Triggered actions taken from the roll dialog have no entry. Defensive Roll, In All This Confusion, Clever Trick, Parry, Advanced Tactics, Repel, Again, The Pack Defends, Pyre, Draconian Guard, Resist the Unnatural, Foresight, Keeper of Order, Turnabout, Harmonize, Take Two!, Deleted Scene and Command change a result with no trace. History needs them as nested rows with the ability's Trigger line (COPY R3).
    6. Passive number changes have no Why. Mark's edge, kit damage and stability bonuses, Field Arsenal's choice, Improved Field Arsenal / Master of Arms / Grandmaster of Arms, Barbed Tail, Hit 'Em Hard!'s surges, Coat the Blade's poison, Stormheart, Psi Boosts and Trained Assassin fold into the roll; the kit bonus is only on the ability card.
    7. Recoveries spent because of a feature show only "Use Recovery". Revitalizing Limerick, Mark's benefit, Mind Game, Stay Strong and Focus!, Mindlink and Too Slow all heal through the same bare line; the reader cannot tell why a Hero regained Stamina.
    8. Automatic damage without text is silent. Stoned's failed-save damage, Mind Snare and Incinerate (not verified), falling after Wings give out, and the missing Continuity Error and Love Song effects. Whether an automatic event is visible depends entirely on whether the content author typed a chatMessage (Ballad of the Beast's surge is logged; Legendary Drum Fill's is not).
