Prototype: Scratch-style authoring that compiles to today's ability data

Drag a block onto the canvas, or click it to add it to the selected script. Drag blocks back here to delete them.

GoblinScript to blocks

Type or paste a formula. It is parsed into blocks, then printed back out, to test that the two views can round-trip without losing meaning. Anything the parser does not understand becomes a grey raw formula block instead of failing.
