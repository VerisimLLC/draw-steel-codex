    History: wording for sign-off¶

    Status 2026-10-27: all signed off, including Round 2 (James: "Yes, those work"). Applied to the prototype the same day. Signed off as listed: N1 (History), N2, T, C (except C4), S, M, E.

    Every user-facing string in the prototype, grouped by surface. Reply with the IDs you want changed; anything not called out is signed off. Ability and trigger names in [brackets] are content, not wording. Rules applied: Hero and Stamina capitalised; condition names capitalised as names ("Slowed ended"), lower case inside sentences ("is slowed"); no pronouns for creatures (names instead).

    Three calls first¶

    N1. The feature's name. Recommended: History. It is the word on the radial slot, the hero card chip and the tray, and it says what it holds without "log" (a text dump, the thing being replaced). Alternatives: keep "Action Log" (familiar to today's users); "Combat History" (wrong outside combat: tests, montages, negotiation). If History: the old name appears once in the rail tooltip for a release, "History (was Action Log)".

    N2. "What happened" sentences.     Round 2: James's four changes (signed off 2026-10-27)¶

    Draw Steel's own wording (Heroes book): you gain and spend surges; each surge spent deals extra damage equal to your highest characteristic score; two or more edges make a double edge; "a double edge and just one bane, the roll is made with one edge, regardless of how many individual edges contribute"; a creature uses a triggered action, and every triggered ability has its own Trigger: line.

    R1. Why lines: the cause first, then what it did (replaces "X, not Y"). The lead names the rule or source in Draw Steel's words; the sentence says what changed, with "instead of" where a number moved. | ID | Lead | Sentence | |---|---|---| | Y1 | Bane from Reactor 1's [Reactor trigger] | It cancelled one of three edges, so the roll stayed at Tier 1 instead of Tier 2. | | Y2 | Cover | A bane on the roll against the Fury only: 13 - 2 = 11, Tier 1 instead of Tier 2. | | Y3 | Damage immunity 2 | Reactor 1 took 7 damage instead of 9. | | Y4 | Fire immunity 3 | Test Monster Z 1 took 4 fire damage instead of 7. The 7 includes +1 from [Elementalist feature] on Fire abilities. | | Y5 | Spent 2 surges | Each dealt 2 extra damage (the Fury's highest characteristic, Might 2). | | Y5b | Spent 2 surges | Each dealt 2 extra damage (Agility 2). One was gained from Trained Assassin and could only be spent on this strike. | | Y6 | Marked by the High Elf Tactician | The strike dealt 4 extra damage. | | Y7 | Fell 4 squares | The fall dealt 6 damage, for 14 instead of 8. Hover here for the side view. | | Y8 | Potency raised with 2 surges | A<1 became A<2, so Reactor 1 could not resist being knocked prone. | | Y9 | Reason 0 / Reason 2 | R<1: the Fury could not resist being slowed. / R<1: the Elementalist resisted. | | Y10 | Immune to frightened | Test Monster Z 1 cannot be frightened. | | Y12 | Area ability | The burst hits every creature in the area, allies included. | | Y13 | Minions | Each minion in this squad has 5 Stamina, so 5 damage killed one. | | Y14 | Healing Grace | It ends one condition: grabbed. | | Y15 | Discipline | The Null gains 1 Discipline when an enemy spends Malice. | | Y16 | (nothing changed it) | Nothing changed the roll or the damage. |

    Round 9: removed edges and banes (signed off 2026-11-08)¶

    Ignore Edges, Ignore Banes, Remove Edge, Remove Bane and the removing half of Edge becomes Bane are listed too, so the card explains an edge that was there but did not count.     Round 10: the Tier line split (signed off 2026-11-08)¶

    A target's sum lists what made its damage, in the order it applied: the tier's own damage (characteristic included), the kit, the roll dialog's damage modifiers, then surges, then immunity or weakness and Taken.
