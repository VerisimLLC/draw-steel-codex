    Action Log redesign - design brief (DRAFT, in discovery)¶

    Status: Phase 1 framing signed; Phase 3 decision map next. Started 2026-10-23.

    1. Framing¶

    • Problem: when something happens that a player or the Director did not follow, they cannot find out what happened quickly. The log is hidden behind a rail button, shows a noisy stream of bespoke cards, and buries the actual outcome (who lost how much Stamina, which condition landed) in hover tooltips.
    • Example question it must answer in seconds: "I did 7 damage, why did the monster only take 5?" -> poison damage, the monster has poison immunity 2.
    • Who asked: David; Ricky raised it too. The dev team rarely refers to the log.
    • Not bound to today's form. Same spirit as the tabletop HUD, which reframed VTT play from map-first to table-first: the table holds the map and other things.
    • Personas:
    • Player who missed something ("why did I lose 14 Stamina?").
    • Director settling a dispute or recovering from a mistake ("did the mark apply?").
    • Set-and-forget player who never opens it.
    • Primary job: recall first. Full-session needs are served as outputs, not as the log's core design (see Open questions).
    • Done looks like: TBD.
    • Pillars (DRAFT, proposed 2026-10-23):
    • Outcomes first, mechanics on request.
    • Answer from where the question comes up (the creature, your hero, the round), not only from a panel. At a physical table nobody keeps a log: memory is spoken and tied to the creature and the moment.
    • Record what happened when it happened; never re-draw history from live state.

    Appendix A - Current-state audit (code, 2026-10-23)¶

    Surface¶

    • ChatPanel/ActionLogPanel.lua: DockablePanel.Register{name = "Action Log"}, a rail panel. Unread badge with a count of entries from other users since the panel was last on screen (actionlog:lastviewed per-game preference).
    • Chrome per entry: CreateActionLogCard - 40px token portrait, name, 6px colour bar (player colour, or hard-coded #AA0000 for monsters), indented 20px when "out of turn".
    • Lazy history: builds the newest 12 entries, loads 12 more on scroll to top.
    • DM-only: right-click Delete Message / Clear Chat / Hide from players; per-roll visibility eye.
    • No search, no filter, no grouping beyond cast-adoption (below).

    Data model¶

    • The log is a filtered view of the chat store: every message that is not plain chat, data, object or a custom message on the "chat" channel.
    • Engine cap: ChatPanel.cs:2193 keeps only the newest 128 messages, shared with chat. One ability cast writes several messages (cast + roll + modifiers, heals, resources, notes), so a busy combat holds a few rounds at most. Full-session search is impossible on this store.
    • Entries store ids, not snapshots: names, portraits and the out-of-turn indent are read from LIVE state at render time. A deleted token makes its entries render as 0x0 (they vanish); a renamed token renames history; "out of turn" is computed against the current initiative, not the one at the time.

    What feeds it¶

    Roll messages (every dice roll: power rolls, tests, damage, initiative, Dice panel, macros, roll-on-table, downtime, montage). A power roll whose castid matches a cast card is nested under it. The power-roll card is also a LIVE interaction surface: amend edge/bane after the roll, tier rows highlight, amendable rows.

    Overlap with newer surfaces¶

    • Timeline/AbilitySidebar.lua + EmbeddedRollDialog.lua: the live ability card and roll dialog, with a read-only copy other players see. The log's "watch it happen" job is now largely done there.

    Dormant: the Battle Log document¶

    • Draw Steel Core Rules/MCDMEncounter.lua:2661-2825: when an encounter ends, the Director's client appends a permanent record to the dsBattleLog shared document (max 200 battles): what was fought, outcome, duration, per-hero headline numbers (damage, taken, prevented, kills, downed). Its own comment calls it "the reviewable campaign history". Nothing outside the file reads it: no UI shows it.
    • The outcome screen (Draw Steel UI/DSVictoryScreen.lua) already turns per-hero stats into awarded roles at the end of combat.

    Style debt¶

    Hard-coded colours (#111111, #AA0000, white, #1194FF, #DE1E47), a bespoke layout per card type, 40px dice faces and 28pt totals beside 11px subtext; not on the STYLE_GUIDE row grammar.

    Appendix B - A real log, read cold (James's screenshot, 2026-10-23)¶

    About nine entries fill a tall panel. Problems, in order of cost to the reader:

    1. Subject and object are ambiguous. "Orc Conduit / Roll Resistance vs Might / 10 damage; slowed (EoT)": did the Orc deal it or take it? Targets are tiny portraits on some cards and absent on others.
    2. The result line is the ability's tier text, not what happened. "5 damage" with three targets: to whom, and how much landed after immunity? Whether "slowed" applied (potency) is not shown.
    3. The biggest things on screen are the least useful: dice faces and a 28pt total. The outcome sits in small text below.
    4. Contradictions: a Free Strike marked "Aborted" still shows a roll and "6 damage"; Free Strike labelled "Main Action".
    5. Duplication: "Reactor 1 / 2 fire damage / 2 fire damage" (author text plus the automatic line).
    6. One action spreads across several cards ("Power Roll Test" card, then a separate "Power Roll Test: Power Roll" card).
    7. The colour bar is red on every entry, so it carries no information. Director-only eye icons sit in every roll.
    8. No round or turn structure is visible in a scroll of this length.

    Appendix C - Why would anyone want a full-session log? (reasoned guesses)¶

    Why Better served by
    Writing session notes or a recap A generated session summary written to a journal document
    "Who carried the fight" / bragging rights An encounter recap (the Battle Log record and outcome-screen stats already exist)
    Catching up after being away from the table or joining late "Since your last turn" catch-up, scoped to your hero
    Disputes about something two rounds ago Recall of the current encounter, searchable by creature
    Resource bookkeeping ("how many Recoveries did I spend?") The hero sheet's state, not a log
    Bug reports and support Developer logging; out of scope for the player-facing design

    Appendix D - Precedent scan (web, 2026-10-23)¶

    Items marked [unverified] rest on search snippets only.

    • Pathfinder Kingmaker / Wrath (Owlcat): one line per action; hover lists every bonus and penalty with its source; Combat / Dialogue / Events tabs; enemy stats gated by knowledge ("?19-34"). Closest precedent for the "why". https://www.gamebanshee.com/kd8qf
    • Baldur's Gate 3: players treat the combat log's math as the source of truth when tooltips were wrong (2024). https://mein-mmo.de/baldurs-gate-3-luegt-seit-monaten-tooltips-falsch/
    • Solasta: a tooltip saying why a roll had advantage was "one of the most requested" features; without it players could not adapt their next turn. https://www.gamebanshee.com/3df88
    • Hearthstone history bar: icons for the last ~7-10 plays, blue border yours, red theirs, hover for detail. Cautionary case: an entry showed cast-time damage (20), not what landed (30). https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/History
    • Into the Breach: prevents the question by telegraphing outcomes in advance.
    • Foundry core: no search; GM export to .txt. Modules fill the gap: Chat Trimmer (combat rounds summarised, originals one click away, full-text search), Damage Log (HP changes with undo; non-owners see less; no "why"), Dice Chronicle (end-of-session stats and awards), and Draw Steel: Battle Log - a direct competitor that groups the chat by battle phase. https://foundryvtt.com/packages/draw-steel-battle-log
    • Roll20: chat persists across sessions; the Chat Archive has no search since 2019. Users: players Ctrl+F the logs "to locate details in the story". https://app.roll20.net/forum/permalink/7658843/
    • D&D Beyond Game Log: campaign-wide, cannot be cleared; complaints from 2021 to 2026.
    • Owlbear Rodeo: deliberately minimal ("Recent Rolls").

    Why people want long history (evidence): finding story details later (Roll20), writing recaps (bloggers scan the roll log for rolls that changed what characters knew, chose, lost or risked), stats and awards (Foundry modules). Recap tools for absent players work from voice transcripts, not the VTT log. Weak or no evidence for streaming or AFK catch-up.

    Gaps nobody fills (opportunity): 1. Dealt vs taken, with the reason ("7 dealt, 5 taken: poison immunity 2"). 2. Sessions as a unit (split, clear, recap by session). 3. Built-in search and filter. 4. Per-creature history ("what happened to this goblin"). 5. Hidden information: what detail each viewer may see. 6. Glanceable and deep together. 7. Log to recap material.

    Appendix E - Option B explored (2026-10-23, provisional)¶

    Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/UpyCsCKpcPuCcsdcrpgzXN (two panels: James's screenshot re-expressed as B, interactive; and the awkward cases). All copy in it is placeholder.

    Anatomy of an entry¶

    • Head: actor portrait, actor, ability, action type, expand caret.
    • One line per target: portrait, name, condition chips (struck through when resisted), a reduction marker when immunity/weakness changed the number, net Stamina change.
    • Cost line when something was spent (Malice, Heroic Resource).
    • Triggered actions nest under the action they interrupted.
    • One click opens the detail: rolls with dice, every edge/bane and its cause, the printed tier text, per-target math ("7 damage - 2 (damage immunity 2) = 5 taken; Stamina 30 -> 25"), potency checks, movement, who rolled and when.
    • Grouped under Round and Turn headings.

    The awkward cases and how B handles them (recommendations)¶

    Case Handling
    Triggered action mid-action Nested under the interrupted action, tagged Triggered
    Effects with no actor (Bleeding, aura damage, save ends) Compact effect row under the turn it happened in
    Resource ticks (start-of-turn Heroic Resource, Malice gain) Compact row under the turn; Malice on the round heading
    Director's manual edits (Stamina, conditions) Compact "Director changed X" row. Not logged today
    Forced movement, winded, dying, killed, minions killed Chips on the target line. Not logged today
    Minion squad One entry for the squad
    Hidden creature name "Unknown creature", as today
    Director-hidden roll Shown only on the Director's view, marked hidden
    Cancelled action with no effect One muted line
    Tests, free dice rolls, out-of-combat play Same row grammar, outside any round
    Sound log (signed HoH feature, per-player opt-in) Kept as a muted compact row for those who opted in

    Tested against the needs¶

    Need B alone
    "I did 7, why did it take 5?" Yes: reduction marker at a glance, math one click away
    "Did the condition apply?" (Director dispute) Yes: chips, resisted ones struck through, potency in the detail
    "What did the monsters do while I was away?" Yes: round/turn headings plus one line per action
    "Why did I lose 14 Stamina?" Partly: needs a per-creature lens (search/filter category)
    Set-and-forget player No: needs outcomes surfaced outside the log (surface category)

    Where B strains¶

    1. An entry is complete only when the action resolves: it needs an in-progress state.
    2. Nesting a trigger needs a link from the triggered cast to the one it interrupted (engine check; the roll dialog's trigger phase is the likely place).
    3. Revert Turn: entries after the checkpoint must be struck or removed.
    4. It needs a structured record per action, written when the action resolves.

    Engine reality for B¶

    • The spine exists: CastActivatedAbilityChatMessage already carries castid, caster, targets, status, and per-target outcomes via RecordTokenMessage (~25 call sites), but as display strings. B makes them structured and snapshots names and portraits.
    • DamageModifierChatMessage is sent from InflictDamageInstance, which already receives symbols.cast, so the reduction can attach to its action.
    • Roll messages are separate chat messages and get pruned at 128; the detail layer must snapshot roll results into the action record rather than point at them.
    • B writes roughly one record per action where today writes three to five messages, so the same cap holds more history (a data-model decision later).
