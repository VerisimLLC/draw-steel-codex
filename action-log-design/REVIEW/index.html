    History: design review before the build (2026-11-04)¶

    Inputs: an independent fresh-eyes review of the design and prototype; a coverage audit of today's Action Log; David's feedback after the demo; a rules review in four parts (rules-review/core.md, classes-1.md, classes-2.md, director.md, about 700 events). Details and code references: BRIEF.md Appendix X and the four rules files.

    Step by step (from 2026-11-05)¶

    James asked to take this in small steps. Step 1 decided the rule below; step 2 sorts each gap by it and says whether the fix is code, data or both; then the fixes and decisions.

    Step 1, decided 2026-11-05: what earns a place. Did it change the table? - Glance: Stamina up or down; a condition or effect starting or ending; moved by someone else, or falling; resources (Heroic Resource, surges, Malice, Hero Tokens, Recoveries); winded, dying, death, minions dying. - Card only: what explains a number (edges and banes, kit bonuses, potency, immunity amounts, which surge went where). - Left out: what changed nothing (walking on your own turn, a trigger that did nothing, a start of turn where nothing happened). - Exception (James, 2026-11-06): a roll the table watched gets a line even if it changed nothing, so a failed save shows, at the end of that turn, with Active on it.

    Step 2: the gaps, one at a time. Gaps 1 and 2 below turned out to share one cause, so there are five: (1) results with no card or authored message; (2) effects ending; (3) winded, dying, death, minion deaths; (4) Malice; (5) passive modifiers unnamed.

    Gap 1, checked in data 2026-11-05. Prompted triggers (the player is asked, e.g. Wellspring of Grace through the shared "Spend Recovery" prompt) already post a card; what they lose is the cause. Truly automatic ones (no question asked) show nothing without a typed message: Righteous Phalanx (15 damage on entering, classes/conduit.yaml:12006), Edict of Unyielding Resolve (10 temporary Stamina each turn, characterongoingeffects/ edict-of-unyielding-resolve.yaml:52), start-of-turn area damage (MCDMCreature.lua:7144). History replaces the Action Log, so today's cards and typed messages have nowhere else to show; History writes its own lines.

    Marker effects (proposed 2026-11-05, James's idea): ongoing effects already carry the editor's "Hidden" checkbox (statusEffect = false, DMHub Compendium/OngoingEffectEditor.lua:182; 83 effects, Righteous Phalanx Target among them), which every token and sheet display honours (DMHub Token UI/TokenUI.lua:337). History leaves Hidden effects out. "Hidden from enemies" (26) follows the token rule. "Hidden on token" (98) is mixed: markers (Moved This Turn, Blitz Tracking, Chain Reaction Target) and real effects (Vanish, Levitate, Rivalry), so History shows them and the pure markers get "Hidden" ticked in data. Withdrawn the same day: "Hidden" also takes an effect off the Tac panel's chips, the only place a Director removes it by hand (James). The markers need their own History signal; the list is in marker-effects.md. Decided 2026-11-05 (James): a new checkbox on ongoing effects, ticked on markers, keeps them out of History and changes nothing else. Its label waits on the feature's name, which James reopened the same day.

    Gap 1 closed 2026-11-05 (James). Code: History records damage, healing, temporary Stamina, conditions and resources where they happen, names the cause, and skips changes that changed nothing (temporary Stamina keeps the greater amount, DSTemporaryHitpoints.lua:6). Lingering area damage goes through one function (creature:AuraDamage, MCDMCreature.lua:7144), so one fix covers Wall of Fire, monster auras and zones. Data: tick "Hidden from History" (COPY.md H1) on the markers in marker-effects.md once James has checked the list. Prompted triggers already post a card; they gain their cause.

    Gap 2, where effects end (checked 2026-11-05). A handful of places, not one per ability: removal (creature:RemoveOngoingEffect/BySeq, RemoveMatchingOngoingEffects, RemoveOngoingEffectsOnTrigger, PurgeCondition: saves, abilities that end a condition, dying, end of encounter, by hand); end of turn and end of round (creature:EndTurn, creature:EndRound, DMHub Game Rules/Creature.lua:11183, :11230); a sustain formula failing (creature:RefreshToken tidy-up, :6134); timers and concentration that lapse quietly (CharacterOngoingEffectInstance:Expired, OngoingEffect.lua:531, filtered out with no removal; History checks them at each turn end). Persistence adds its own piece (creature:EndPersistentAbilityById, MCDMCreature.lua:7512) for the cause and for walls and areas, which are not effects on a creature. Gap 2 closed 2026-11-05 (James): the broad fix plus the persistence piece; effects that end together are grouped (one line per turn end; one closing entry for the encounter, listing what ended by creature). Wording in the later sign-off round.

    Gap 3, winded, dying, death, minion deaths (checked 2026-11-05). Detected in two places, taking damage (MCDMCreature.lua:6806-6871) and Stamina set directly, e.g. a hand edit (:7069-7083); each fires an event and a sound, never a log entry. Confirmed minion deaths fire once per batch (squadminiondeaths, :6109). Proposal: captions on the row that caused it (T14, signed off), a line when nothing did, recovery shown too, minions only once confirmed. Gap 3 closed 2026-11-05 (James), as proposed. Code only.

    Gap 4, Malice (checked 2026-11-05). Correction to section 1: spending Malice on an ability already posts a card everyone sees (MaliceSpentChatMessage, DSResources.lua:272). Every other change goes only to the Director's counter tooltip. All changes pass through CharacterResource.SetMalice (DSResources.lua:79) with a reason already attached (start of combat, each round's gain, traits, "Manually set", end of combat), so the fix is one hook. Open: the rules leave it to the Director whether players see Malice (CRM:785-790). Who sees what today (from code, 2026-11-05): gains are Director-only (the action-bar counter's tooltip, the Encounter Wrangler). Players see every spend card, and a Malice tracker the Director places on the table as public (DMHub Game Hud/TableDice.lua:2316), whose hover shows the same history with reasons (:2641). So the codex's "Director's choice" is that tracker; the earlier "on by default" proposal was withdrawn. Revised proposal: keep today's split in History (players see spends, the Director sees every change); sharing stays the public tracker's job. Gap 4 closed 2026-11-05 (James), with the revised proposal. Code only (one hook in SetMalice); no new setting.

    Gap 5, passive numbers (checked 2026-11-05). Card only by the rule (they explain a number). Most names already exist in three places: roll modifiers kept by the roller (rollProperties.modifiersUsed, DSRollDialog.lua:3963), kit bonuses written onto the ability ("Includes +2 damage from {kit} kit", MCDMKit.lua:475), immunity and weakness in the damage code. Left over: changes made outside a roll, e.g. healing halved through the "Stamina Regain Halved" attribute (ActivatedAbility.lua:5089), where the code knows the change but not which effect caused it. Possible data side: a bonus written straight into a formula instead of a named modifier shows as an unexplained total. Generic answer (James asked for no per-ability code, 2026-11-05): creature:DescribeModifications (DMHub Game Rules/Creature.lua:7147) and DescribeModificationsToNamedCustomAttribute (MCDMCreature.lua:222) already list every modifier that changed an attribute, with the value before and after; the Hero sheet's stat sources use them (EotwHeroSheetData.lua:424-477). So each place a number lands (the gap 1 hooks) asks those for the attributes it read. Any ability, item or DLC built from modifiers is named with no new code; only content that bypasses modifiers (a script editing numbers, a bonus typed into a formula) cannot be. Gap 5 closed 2026-11-05 (James), with the generic approach. Step 2 is done: all five gaps closed. Still open from it: James checks marker-effects.md; the new line wording (trigger results, temporary Stamina, effects ending and grouped endings, recovering from winded or dying) goes in one sign-off round. Next: the fixes in section 2, one at a time, then the decisions in section 3 (decision 1 is mostly settled by the gaps; its open part is whether F8 and F9 join the first build).

    1. The big finding: History has to record at the source, not from chat¶

    Most of what players can't explain never reaches today's log, for a handful of reasons: - Automatic triggers post no ability card (TriggeredAbility:ShowChatMessageOnCast returns not mandatory, TriggeredAbility.lua:2644; mandatory is the default). Their per-target results are written onto that card, so with no card they are dropped (ActivatedAbility.lua:3174). Their damage still shows when the author filled in the damage behavior's chat message (AbilityDamage.lua:56), and immunity/weakness always posts its own line. (Correction 2026-11-05: an earlier wording said they post nothing.) - Chat and the log are split by a filter: everything that is not plain chat goes to the Action Log (ChatPanel/ActionLogPanel.lua:31), so nothing log-worthy is stuck in chat. - Damage, healing, temporary Stamina and resource lines appear only when the content author typed a chat message on that behavior. Flat rule-text damage ("2 fire damage") posts nothing. So coverage depends on authoring, ability by ability. - Nothing records an effect ending: end of turn, saves, dying, end of combat, a sustain condition lost, persistence broken (the Elementalist's 5 x Reason rule only shows floating text, elementalist.yaml:4869). - Winded, dying, death and minion deaths are events with sounds, never entries. Minion deaths are chosen by whoever hit the squad, so History must record the confirmed deaths. - Malice changes are recorded only in a hover tooltip on the Director's counter. - Passive numbers (kit bonuses, captain, auras, the Mark) fold into rolls unnamed.

    Proposal R: record at the source. History does not read chat. Each family of event is written where the game already handles it, so every ability and trait is covered without per-ability authoring, and the old chat cards can stay as they are. | Family | Recorded at | Shows as | |---|---|---| | F1 Actions, prompted triggers | ActivatedAbility:Cast | action row | | F2 Automatic triggers (traits, auras, class features) | the triggered cast, mandatory too | nested under its cause, else a line naming the feature | | F3 Damage, healing, temporary Stamina from any source (auras, terrain, start/end of turn, falls, collisions, hand edits) | the damage, heal and temporary Stamina code | numbers on the action, or a line with its source | | F4 Effects and conditions starting and ending, with why | inflict / remove condition and ongoing effect, persistence end, end of combat, dying | condition badges, "Ended" lines | | F5 Thresholds: winded, dying, death, confirmed minion deaths, strained, rampage, captain changes | the existing creature events | captions and lines | | F6 Resources with a structured cause: Heroic Resource, surges, Malice, Hero Tokens, Recoveries, Victories, losses at end of combat | the resource code (it already keeps a stat history) | lines, chips | | F7 Movement: push, pull, slide, stability, teleport, falls, collisions, wall breaks, with the path | AbilityRelocateCreature | captions, side view, map arrow | | F8 Rolls and play without an action: tests, montages, negotiation, respite, downtime, Dice panel, tables | the roll and those panels | compact rows | | F9 Director actions: reinforcements, villain actions, overrides, hand edits, reveals | the Director's tools | lines (players see what was visible) | | F10 Named modifiers for Why lines | roll dialog modifiersUsed, the damage code | Why lines | Recommendation: F1-F7 and F10 in the first build (every combat); F8 and F9 next.

    Font check for wording (2026-11-06, read from the TextMeshPro assets). Berling is a static atlas. Not available: the arrow U+2192 and less-or-equal U+2264 (no label font's table has them; Berlingske Regular's fallback Newzald-Medium is dynamic and its source has them, but Bold has no fallbacks, so it can't be relied on). DrawSteelGlyphs has no arrow (it has the tier glyphs). Labels have no inline images, so a Phosphor arrow can't sit inside a sentence. Available: middle dot, en dash, times, single guillemet, curly quotes. Minus U+2212 is Regular-only through a fallback, so bold numbers in the Codex use a plain "-". Lua source stays ASCII, so these go in as byte escapes. This hits T5 ("Malice +{n} → {n}") and the card's "Stamina a → b" notes.

    • B1 Numbers on the glance are what landed: a face shows the total taken including nested triggers; folds show damage and healing separately; captions use taken numbers.
    • B2 An actor face on a row whose actor isn't the turn's only creature (a Director's group turn); an action with no cause sits as a line in the turn it happened in.
    • B3 Nothing moves under the reader: no folding while the pointer is on the tray or a card is open; the selected turn never folds; when scrolled up, hold position and show "{n} new below".
    • B4 Signals that are not hover-only or colour-only: the side view gets an icon button on the movement caption (drop "Hover here for the side view"); hovering it also draws the movement arrow on the map (David); a "Resisted" caption; duplicate portraits numbered; the gold badge carries a shape, not colour alone. (The hero card's hover chip stays: James's constraint.)
    • B5 One visibility rule: the record shows players what they saw live; spoiler modifiers stay redacted until revealed; anything not shown live (a monster's characteristic in a Why line, minion Stamina) follows Monster Info.
    • B6 Scale: a "+N" face when a strip or fold is full; identical outcomes grouped ("Goblins 1-4 each took 6"); results built lazily.
    • B7 Font Size and narrow windows: the card opens on the side with room, or inside the tray when there is none; glance text never smaller than 11 px at 100%.
    • B8 Wording: one tier label everywhere ("Tier 1", the badge showing "<=11" under it only on the roll card); "Nothing in this encounter matches" becomes "Nothing matches"; search accepts "turn 15"; "Active" shows the same way in tray and card. New wording in this block for sign-off: "{n} new below", "Nothing matches "{search}"."

    3. Decisions, one per round¶

    1. What History records (Proposal R above), and the first-build scope.
    2. Defaults, the rail and notifications: History on its own rail slot, not in the Chat group (David); what is on by default for players and the Director; the brief preview beside the button or block when History is closed, reusing the chat speech bubble, with a setting (David); the badge when the tray is off the table; the new-rows marker on an open tray.
    3. Several maps, each in its own combat: one History per map, or one that follows the focused map.
    4. Revert Turn, undo and the Director's tools: strike-through or erase on revert; Delete, Hide from players, Clear; Undo on resource lines.
    5. Out of combat and keeping history: how play without rounds groups; how long History keeps things between encounters and sessions; the ability card on hover; content authors' own lines.

    4. Possible rules bugs found along the way (not History work)¶

    The rules agents noted implementation differences from the rules. None are verified; each needs checking against the book before anyone changes code. Lists: core.md "Surprises", classes-1.md "Rules deviations noticed", classes-2.md "Surprises and data issues", director.md "Gaps in rules automation". Examples: no damage for being thrown cleanly through a wall; save-ends effects continuing after combat; Prophecy marked Gold with no modifiers; Quick and Brutal granting a maneuver where the rule grants a main and a move action; Materialize using a flat 2 instead of Reason; features not implemented (Warmaster, Masterwork, Templar, Oracular Visions and others).
