# The Beastheart Class

A beastheart travels with a ferocious beast companion -- not a trained pet, but an untamed creature bound by a primordial connection. As battle rages, the companion may succumb to a blood-soaked rampage, lashing out at enemies and allies alike. The beastheart is a **master class**, intended for players familiar with the game.

---

## Basics

**Starting Characteristics:** Might 2, Intuition 2, plus one of:
- 2, -1, -1
- 1, 1, -1
- 1, 0, 0

**Weak Potency:** Might - 2
**Average Potency:** Might - 1
**Strong Potency:** Might

**Starting Stamina at 1st Level:** 21
**Stamina Gained at 2nd and Higher Levels:** 12
**Recoveries:** 12

**Skills:** Animal Handling (automatic), plus any two from exploration or intrigue skill groups.
*(Quick Build: Animal Handling, Navigate, Track.)*

## 1st-Level Features

### Wild Nature (Subclass)

Choose one wild nature. Each grants a skill and determines many features gained at higher levels.

- **Guardian:** Fearless defender of the pack. Grants **Read Person** skill.
- **Prowler:** Unseen ambusher from the shadows. Grants **Hide** skill.
- **Punisher:** Overwhelms with brute force. Grants **Endurance** skill.
- **Spark:** Elemental storm magic crackles from hands and claws. Grants **Magic** skill.

*(Quick Build: Punisher.)*

---

### Companion

Choose one companion species. *(Quick Build: Wolf.)*

The companion's Stamina maximum equals the beastheart's Stamina maximum. The companion has no Recoveries -- when an effect allows the companion to spend a Recovery, it spends one of the beastheart's.

#### Companion Rules

- **Companion Death:** Can become dying at 0 Stamina and die at negative half Stamina, like a hero.
- **Companion Actions:** Takes its turn as part of the beastheart's turn. Start/end of beastheart's turn = start/end of companion's turn. Each takes their own move action. One triggered action per round (either). Main action and maneuver are shared -- if one takes a main action, the other takes the maneuver.
- **Ranged Free Strikes:** Companion has none.
- **Shared Maneuvers:** When either uses Catch Breath, Escape Grab, Hide, or Stand Up, partner can use same as a free triggered action.
- **Shared Abilities:** Beastheart keyword = beastheart only. Companion keyword = companion only. "You" in ability text = whoever uses it; "partner" = whoever didn't. Inside companion stat blocks, "you" = beastheart. "You both"/"you each" = both.
- **Shared Senses:** Within 1 mile, telepathic communication (images and feelings).
- **Shared Skills:** Companion has all beastheart's skills, vice versa (physiology permitting).
- **Shared Space:** Can move freely through and stop in each other's spaces.
- **Shared Perks, Titles, Complications:** Companion shares benefits/drawbacks that logically affect an animal.
- **Surges:** Shared surge pool. When effect would grant surges to both, gain surges only once.
- **Changing Companion:** As a respite activity, release current companion and gain a new one.
- **One Hero:** Beastheart + companion count as one hero for difficulty purposes.
- **Montage Tests:** Share one turn; typically the beastheart takes it.

### Ferocity (Heroic Resource)

**In Combat:** At the start of a combat encounter, gain ferocity equal to your Victories. At the start of each of your turns, gain 1d3 ferocity. Additionally, the first time each combat round that a creature adjacent to your companion takes damage, you gain 2 ferocity. You lose remaining ferocity at the end of the encounter.

**Outside of Combat:** Can't gain ferocity outside of combat, but can use heroic abilities and effects that cost ferocity without spending it. After using an ability or effect outside of combat that costs ferocity, that same ability can't be used again outside of combat until you earn 1+ Victories or finish a respite. For variable or unlimited ferocity costs outside of combat, use as if you had spent ferocity equal to your Victories.

### Rampage

Whenever you or your companion spends ferocity, your companion gains rampage equal to the ferocity spent. Your companion loses their rampage and its effects at the end of an encounter.

Your companion doesn't spend rampage to activate abilities. When your companion gains 8 rampage, they are **rampaging**. Effects are cumulative.

## 10th-Level Features

### Characteristic Increase
Your and your companion's Might and Intuition scores increase to 5.

### Companion Advancement Feature
Your companion gains the level 10 advancement feature granted by their stat block.

### Final Evolution
When you gain ferocity at the start of each of your turns during combat, you gain 2d3 + 1 ferocity instead of 1d3 + 1.

### Perk
Gain one exploration, interpersonal, or intrigue perk of your choice.

### Ferox
You have an epic resource called ferox. Each time you finish a respite, you gain ferox equal to the XP you gain. You can spend ferox on your abilities as if it were ferocity.

Additionally, you can spend 1 ferox as a free maneuver to allow you and your companion to each take a main action on your turn, instead of a main action and a maneuver. On that turn, the ferocity cost of your heroic abilities is reduced by 1.

Ferox remains until you spend it.

### Rampage Improvement
Your Rampage feature provides additional effects when your companion has 24 or more rampage.

### Skill
Gain one skill of your choice.
