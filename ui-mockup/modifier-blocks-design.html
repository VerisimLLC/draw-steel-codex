Modifier Blocks

How class features, kits, ancestries, titles and effects would be edited with the same sentence blocks as the simple ability editor. Every example below is a real feature from the compendium.

1 · Opening a feature

The feature's text on the left, what it does as sentences in the middle

Elementalist, Enchantment of Battle. Its two modifiers open as two sentences. The editor compares them with the feature's own text and points out what the text says but the sentences don't.

The feature (book text, saved wording)

Enchantment of Battle

Elementalist · Enchantment

You can wear light armor and wield light weapons effectively, even though you don't have a kit. While you wear light armor, you gain a +3 bonus to Stamina, and that bonus increases by 3 at 4th, 7th, and 10th levels. While you wield a light weapon, you gain a +1 damage bonus with weapon abilities, including free strikes.

If you have a kit, you can't take this enchantment.
